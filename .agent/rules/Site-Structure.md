# site-structure.md
Job: what the public site shows and where. Product facts are in catalogue.md.

- Use the primary navigation Shop Paints, Colours, Help Me Choose (guide and calculator), Commercial and Bulk, and Contact and Delivery. Why: it is the confirmed, deliberately short set.
- Keep the admin area out of public navigation. Why: staff tools are not for customers.
- On mobile, keep three actions visible on every public page: quote request, WhatsApp and call. Take both WhatsApp numbers from config. Beside WhatsApp, say who monitors it and when, using names and hours from config. Why: customers need to know who will answer and when.
- Put these in the footer: About, Projects, Privacy, Sales Terms, and Returns and Damaged Goods. Show Projects only when genuine case studies exist (G9). Link a policy only when its text is approved (G4). Why: an unapproved policy page is a published promise.
- Build the homepage with real packaging and project photos, a plain explanation of what ORACHIN Paints makes, featured categories and two main actions, "Shop Paints" and "Request a Quote". State that painting application services are offered. Show delivery information with no nationwide promise (G8). Why: these are the confirmed homepage needs.
- Build a service page for painting application services. Invent no credentials. Why: the owner confirmed the service but supplied no credentials.
- Show only genuine photos, projects, reviews, addresses and credentials. Publish service-area content only for areas the business serves. Keep synthetic fixtures out of production data and builds. Why: fabricated proof damages trust and may break advertising rules.
- Write unique page titles and descriptions, readable URLs, a sitemap and canonical links. Use structured data only for facts visible on the page. For a missing or archived product, show helpful navigation or a redirect. Why: search listings must not contradict the page.
- Required tests: navigation labels match this file. The three mobile actions appear on every public page. Projects is hidden without genuine content. No unapproved policy page is reachable. No fixture data is in the production build. Why: these prove the rules above hold.
